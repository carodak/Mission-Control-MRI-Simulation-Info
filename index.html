Official information page for the Mission Control VR MRI simulation app.

Overview

Mission Control is a free VR MRI simulation app. This page provides official information about it.

You will embark on an immersive, story-driven VR adventure that familiarizes you with the MRI procedure. Discover every step of the MRI journey in a playful, interactive way. You will step into the shoes of a special astronaut with one mission: to reach the Moon. To succeed, you must board your spaceship and stay on course at all times. Our Mission Control center will be watching over you! We will send real-time feedback if you move and your ship drifts off its path. Are you ready, astronaut?
